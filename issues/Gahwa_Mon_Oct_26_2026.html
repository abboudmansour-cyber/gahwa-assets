The Lead
​
Houthis Keep Targeting Riyadh
The Saudi-led coalition intercepted a ballistic missile fired toward Riyadh and a drone in the Riyadh region. The Houthis claimed missile strikes on King Khalid International Airport. The Saudi Civil Defense says it intercepted two missiles launched toward Makkah and Madinah, with debris falling in Makkah, Jeddah, Taif, and Rabigh. The Houthis deny targeting the two holy cities.

Over the weekend, the Houthis say they carried out seven operations against Saudi airports, Aramco facilities in Yanbu, the Rabigh refinery, crude pumping stations, and the east-west pipeline. Marine insurance premiums are running 10 to 15 times normal levels, and bunker fuel has at times tripled in price.

The Kingdom has postponed its Future Investment Initiative, the UN World Data Forum, and the Saudi Statistics Forum to 2027. The cost of doing business in the Kingdom now includes a war premium that no amount of Vision 2030 planning can fully price. Reportedly, the lead story is that Saudi Arabia's economic calendar, from investment conferences to flights, now bends around Houthi missile trajectories.

02MENA scaleup debt gap
MENA Scaleups Have a Debt Wall
MENA equity funding fell 22% year-on-year in the first half of 2026 to $1.35 billion. International investors cut deployment by nearly two-thirds, and local investors supplied 81% of all venture capital, the highest share in five years.

The scaleup capital stack is shifting from equity to debt. Tamara's Shariah-compliant asset-backed facility is up to $2.4 billion, and Tabby's $700 million receivables securitisation set a regional precedent.

The GCC has an estimated $250 billion SME credit gap. The money is there, but it now comes with covenants and repayment dates instead of headline valuations. The capital stack now comes with an actual stack of covenants.
Source: Wamda

03Foldable problem
iPhone Duo Buyers Report Cracks
Apple's first foldable, the iPhone Duo, is drawing complaints of cracked screens and display failures hours after use, with damage concentrated around the hinge. UAE pricing runs from Dh8,499 to Dh13,599, and initial stock sold out on strong pre-orders.

Apple is reportedly sending damaged units to its engineering teams to find the cause. A foldable that cracks out of the box is not a feature. Some buyers are getting a closer look at the hinge than they paid for.

04Private market workflow
FinBursa 360 AI Platform
FinBursa launched FinBursa 360, an AI-powered platform for private markets firms. It brings five specialized AI agents and a coordinating orchestrator to deal execution and investor relations. Founded in 2024 by a former BlackRock director, the company says firms can go live in days.

The platform is ISO 27001:2022 certified and SOC 2 Type 1 attested. This is a quiet push to automate the unglamorous plumbing of private capital. Two certifications and five agents are coming for the unglamorous plumbing.

The AI Race
​
Apollo: AI Still Pays Zero
Apollo's chief economist says artificial intelligence has delivered 'zero financial payoff' to corporate America outside the tech sector. The finding echoes a McKinsey statistic that 80% of AI users feel more productive, but only 6% of companies see a meaningful profit impact. The timing is awkward: Big Tech earnings week opens with Alphabet, Microsoft, and Amazon.

AI spending is pushing several tech giants into negative free cash flow. The Gulf is betting over $100 billion that physical compute, the data centers and chips themselves, will be the winning layer. An economist saying AI has not yet paid off outside tech is the pause the region's data center faith does not want to hear. The buildings are going up while the global economy asks when the brains will pay rent. If the productivity arrives late, Gulf data centers will charge by the watt instead of the insight.
Business
​
Folk Maritime CEO Targets Doubling
Folk Maritime, owned by Saudi Arabia's PIF, is sticking to its target of doubling its business within three years. CEO Poul Hestbaek said the plan relies on secondhand vessels, charters, partners, and newbuild orders toward the end of the period. The plan leans on secondhand vessels and charters, which confirms doubling does not require being new.
Adnoc details 2027 oil and gas tenders
Adnoc is preparing a new wave of tenders for 2027 covering offshore sour gas, an export gas pipeline, and enhanced oil recovery projects. The company also plans to cut tender cycle times by 40% and evaluate bids from November 1 on a 70% commercial and 30% in-country value basis.
Dubai's Emirates NBD seeks $500 mn Asian loan
Emirates NBD is seeking a loan of at least $500 million from Asian banks, with an option to upsize to $1 billion. The five-year tranche is at 90 basis points over SOFR and a seven-year tranche at 110 basis points, with Mizuho Bank underwriting.
Masdar prices $600 mn five-year blue bond
Masdar priced a $600 million five-year blue bond at 115 basis points over US Treasuries, tighter than the initial guidance of about 130 basis points. Orders topped $1.4 billion, more than twice the issue size, and the notes list on the LSE International Securities Market.
