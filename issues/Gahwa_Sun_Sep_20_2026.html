The Lead
​
Houthis Strike Riyadh, officials say
A Yemeni resident of Saudi Arabia was killed by debris from an intercepted drone over Taif, according to reports, the first death reported since fighting intensified last week. The Saudi-led coalition confirmed its air force destroyed a ballistic missile aimed at Riyadh at dawn, marking the first time the capital has been targeted in this escalation, according to the coalition.

The attack came hours after a fire broke out near Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport, where a fuel tank bearing the Aramco logo burned. FlightRadar24 listed the airport under its maximum disruption index before traffic resumed. The Houthis claimed they hit "sensitive sites" in Riyadh and Aramco facilities in Yanbu, according to the group, while Saudi Arabia carried out 26 airstrikes on the group over the weekend.

The UN Security Council condemned the attacks and affirmed Saudi Arabia's right to self-defense. China asked Tehran to help rein in the Houthis after Riyadh appealed for help. The attack reportedly lands just days after Washington cleared a $24.3 billion sale of 48 F-35 fighters to the Kingdom. That package now reads less like a routine arms deal and more like a delivery the buyer cannot take soon enough. Pakistan's army chief warned his country could defend Saudi Arabia if the mutual-defense clause is invoked, and Turkey's foreign minister said Ankara is ready to help with "technical issues." The next two weeks will show whether that help is needed.

02Dollar diplomacy
Turkey revokes Bank Mellat license
The Turkish banking regulator revoked the operating license of the Istanbul branch of Iranian lender Bank Mellat, ending its 44-year presence in the country. The move was published in the Official Gazette on Saturday.

The Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency cited risks to depositors' rights and financial system stability. The move follows intensified US Treasury enforcement targeting cross-border trade with Iran, part of a campaign called Operation Economic Outcast.

Earlier this month, US authorities sanctioned Turkish investment bank Golden Global Investment Bank over alleged financial ties to Iran's IRGC. The US campaign forces regional trade hubs to either enforce Western compliance standards or risk exclusion from the dollar system.

03No Change of Address
Juffali denies relocation reports
E.A. Juffali & Brothers Group categorically denied reports it plans to relocate operations or offices outside Saudi Arabia. The group said its headquarters, operations and investments remain in the Kingdom with no plans to change.

Founded in 1936, the group said it has grown alongside Saudi Arabia for eight decades and remains committed to investing in line with Vision 2030. It denied that Chairman Khaled Ahmed Juffali made any statement about a relocation, calling remarks attributed to him "fabricated."

The group reserved the right to take legal action against anyone who deliberately publishes false information about it.

04ADES Deal Done
ADES completes Saipem acquisition
ADES Saudi, a subsidiary of Tadawul-listed ADES Holding, has completed its acquisition of Saudi Arabian Saipem. The transaction began in June and is estimated to be valued at about $285 million.

Saudi Arabian Saipem operates a fleet of five jack-up rigs. The acquisition adds to ADES Holding's growing portfolio of oil and gas drilling assets in the Kingdom.

The deal strengthens ADES Holding's position as a leading domestic drilling contractor at a time when Saudi Arabia is expanding its oil and gas production capacity.
​
Deadline extended
QatarEnergy extends North Field West bidding time
QatarEnergy has given bidders more time on North Field West topsides. The tender covers the EPCI of four production deck modules to support output from the North Field reservoirs.

The extension gives contractors more time to prepare bids for one of the largest LNG projects in the Gulf. The modules will provide additional gas feed for the North Field West LNG project.

The North Field expansion is central to Qatar's plan to remain the world's largest LNG exporter.

The AI Race
​
Trump's AI Force lands before any law
Trump has announced an 'AI Force' unit and promised a 'tsar' to police it, after major sector figures publicly warned about the technology's dangers. A bipartisan bill requiring an AI 'kill switch' stalled shortly after introduction in Congress. Anthropic has partnered with Accenture on third-party evaluations of frontier models, with each side committing $1 billion over five years.

The AI industry now faces a moment where governments are moving faster than legislation. The new unit gives Trump a way to shape policy without waiting for Congress, while the stalled bill shows how hard binding rules are to pass. Accenture's evaluation deal suggests companies are building oversight themselves rather than waiting for laws. Gulf AI buyers will face a new US regulatory gate before they even get to the chips.
Business
​
Aramco shifts 60m barrels through Ras Tanura
Aramco sold roughly 60 million barrels from Ras Tanura for ship-to-ship transfer off Sohar, Oman. The move lifts Gulf exports back to 1, 1.5 million barrels per day after a pipeline strike. Supertanker freight rates have hit record levels. The reroute keeps crude moving without waiting for repairs.
Safqah Capital eyes a listing
Safqah Capital aims to list in the next few years, CEO Abdullah Alsubaie told Argaam. The two-year-old Riyadh-based company finances real estate developers through sukuk and has financed almost SAR 5 billion worth of development. It raised $15.2 million in a seed round led by Shorooq in February.
Kuwait pauses its $3.3bn gas project
Kuwait postponed the contractor meeting for a $3.3 billion gas project. The project is expected to be the country's biggest oil and gas sector contract award in more than a decade. The new meeting date has not been announced. The delay gives contractors more time but signals a measured approach from Kuwait.
Lendo and Quantic open a $200m SME window
Lendo partnered with Quantic Financial Solutions on an institutional capital programme of up to SAR750 million for Saudi SME working capital financing. The programme was announced at Money20/20 Middle East in Riyadh. Lendo, founded in 2019, is a Shariah-compliant debt crowdfunding platform regulated by SAMA. Quantic is a Vienna-headquartered asset manager.
TotalEnergies signs Venezuela return
TotalEnergies SE signed a memorandum of understanding on Saturday, setting the French company up for a return to operations in Venezuela. The deal marks the latest energy pact between foreign players and Caracas' interim administration after Washington's ouster of Nicolas Maduro in January.

Doha's Mariachi night
The Qatar Philharmonic Orchestra played a 'Sounds of Mexico' concert as part of the Qatar, Canada and Mexico 2026 Year of Culture. It brought trumpets and strings to Doha's cultural calendar. The Year of Culture is becoming a cornerstone of Qatar's soft power strategy, pairing music with trade partners. It turns a concert hall into a quiet diplomatic channel.
