Flydubai Suspends Israel Flights After Attack, Officials Say
Flydubai suspended all flights to and from Israel until further notice after a September 30 incident on flight FZ1073 in which, according to officials, an Omani co-pilot stabbed the Indian captain. The Airbus A380-800 made an emergency landing in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia after the crew transmitted squawk code 7500, the international signal for unlawful interference.

Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu said the co-pilot had undergone "Islamist radical indoctrination," and UAE authorities are investigating a possible terror link. The captain and passengers overpowered the suspect before the landing. Both crew members were treated for injuries.

The UAE opened a formal investigation into the incident. The flight, carrying approximately 180 passengers, was later flown to Tel Aviv on a second flydubai aircraft.

This is the second recent aviation emergency involving a Gulf carrier after an Emirates flight to London diverted to Frankfurt for a medical emergency. The suspension of flydubai's Israel route removes a key Gulf-Israel air link at a time of heightened regional tension.

02Everyone outspends everyone
Abu Dhabi Funds Drive Half of MENA Dealmaking
Abu Dhabi's sovereign funds drove 45% of MENA sovereign dealmaking in the first nine months of 2026, led by Mubadala at USD 26.2 billion, per Global SWF's 2026 MENA Playbook. PIF followed at USD 14.0 billion, ADIA at USD 12.2 billion, and L'imad at USD 10.8 billion.

Three Abu Dhabi funds accounted for almost half of the USD 102 billion MENA sovereign investors deployed across 245 transactions. MENA sovereign funds accounted for 39% of all global state-owned investor dealmaking.

Mubadala is projected to hit USD 35 billion this year versus an average of USD 21.7 billion. Technology, including AI rounds, made up 28% of deal volume and about 30% of value.

The Abu Dhabi funds are on pace to finish the year with USD 136 billion deployed, as the emirate's capital becomes a defining force in global markets. Abu Dhabi's sovereign capital is now the single most important source of Gulf money in global markets, and its allocation choices will shape the region's portfolio for years.

03Three kings of Hollywood
Paramount Deal Closes Oct 6 With USD 52B Debt
Paramount is locked in for a Gulf-backed Warner Bros. Discovery takeover, with a closing date of October 6 and a debt package priced at roughly USD 52 billion in bonds and loans. The USD 30 billion investment-grade portion drew more than USD 109 billion in orders, 3.6x the planned size.

L'imad, PIF, and QIA are putting up about USD 24 billion in equity. After closing, L'imad will hold 12.8% non-voting equity and PIF 15.1%.

Paramount faced USD 7 million in daily delay payments starting September 30, which focused minds. According to reports, this is the largest Gulf-backed Hollywood transaction on record, and the Gulf's three biggest funds now own a controlling stake in a major US studio. Gulf capital now owns a controlling stake in a major Hollywood studio, and the next test is whether the new ownership can turn around the merged company's balance sheet.

barq closed a USD 329.5 million Series A at a USD 1.85 billion valuation, and Tabby raised USD 233 million at a USD 6.5 billion valuation. Network International went live with merchant acquiring in Saudi Arabia in September, nearly three years after receiving its SAMA licence.

Saudi fintech is scaling from a startup story into a financing infrastructure story, and the next phase will hinge on whether new licences turn into actual lending and payment flows. Saudi fintech is no longer just a number of startups; it is becoming the plumbing for SME lending and merchant payments.

04Saudi budget
Saudi Deficit to Overshoot Budget by 80B Riyals
Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Finance expects a 2026 budget deficit of 245 billion riyals (USD 65 billion, or 4.9% of GDP), against a budgeted 165 billion riyals (USD 44 billion). That is a shortfall nearly 50% larger than planned.

Revenue is expected to come in 3.7% higher than budgeted, but expenditure is projected to run 9.3% higher. Real GDP is forecast to contract 3.6% in 2026, while real non-oil GDP is projected to grow 3.2%.

The 2027 deficit is expected to narrow to 191 billion riyals. Government debt remains relatively low at around 35% of GDP, giving the Kingdom room to borrow.

Saudi Arabia's wider-than-budgeted deficit means more debt issuance is likely next year, even as the Kingdom keeps spending to hit Vision 2030 targets. Expect more Saudi bond issuance next year as the Kingdom funds a deficit that is running well ahead of plan.

Google Returns With Argon
Google unveiled Gemini 4 Argon, a frontier model it says beats GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 on 13 of 19 benchmarks. Argon hit 77.9% on DeepSWE, a real-world coding test, and opened at No. 1 on Arena's text leaderboard. Access starts with vetted cybersecurity teams through the Fairwind Program. API pricing begins at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

The model's one-million-token output limit is a leap from the previous 64,000. That changes what a single request can do, from a whole codebase review to a full security audit in one pass. Google spent most of 2026 sidelined after scrapping Gemini 3.5 Pro, so Argon is its comeback entry. Bloomberg reported internal doubts about the coding results, which Google rejected. Gulf AI labs and security teams that get Fairwind approval gain an early edge, but everyone else is waiting at the gate.

The Last Sip
​
What does the phrase 'squawk 7500' actually mean?
The answer
It is the international transponder code a pilot sets to signal unlawful interference, a hijacking. The code appears on the air traffic controller's screen and immediately alerts them that the aircraft needs priority handling. The flydubai crew transmitted it before landing in Tabuk. The calmest way a plane can tell the ground it is having the worst day of its flight.
