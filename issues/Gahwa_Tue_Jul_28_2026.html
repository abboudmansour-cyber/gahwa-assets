For Gulf producers, the drop is a double-edged sword: it relieves pressure on domestic fuel costs and imports while slicing the revenue windfall that has cushioned state budgets. Saudi Trade Surplus Jumps 329% in May
Saudi Arabia's merchandise trade surplus widened 328.8% year-on-year in May to a multi-year high, as oil export revenues climbed 19.5% and imports contracted sharply by the same percentage.

Oil's share of total exports swelled to 75.6% from 65.7% a year earlier, while non-oil exports including re-exports slumped 26.1%. The figures underscore an economy still held aloft by crude while its non-oil ambitions took a backseat, a reminder that even Vision 2030 runs on petrol.

China remained the top export destination at 12.3% of the total, followed by South Korea and the UAE, and was also the largest source of imports at 22.0%. The plunging import bill, down to $14.8 billion, pointed to softer domestic demand and tighter liquidity. The wide surplus gives Saudi policymakers near-term fiscal breathing room but signals that non-oil trade momentum stalled just as the kingdom pushes to diversify its revenue base.

Brookfield to Deploy $2B with PIF
A $2 billion private equity fund anchored by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund will target half of its investments inside the kingdom, as Brookfield deepens its Gulf bet.

The Canadian asset manager held a first close on Brookfield Middle East Partners, committing $500 million of its own capital, and will focus on buyouts and minority growth equity across financial services, consumer, industrials, and healthcare. Brookfield has managed $16 billion in regional assets since 2015 and will launch the Brookfield Academy in Saudi Arabia to train local investment professionals. The fund's structure signals that the smartest route to Gulf deal flow is to staff up locally, not just write a cheque.

AI & TECH
Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 Tops AI Benchmarks
Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 late Friday, a model that matches its pricier sibling Fable 5 on coding and knowledge work but costs half as much to run.

The model scored 30.2% on the notoriously difficult ARC-AGI 3 reasoning test, more than tripling the previous 7.8% record, sending rival AI labs scrambling back to the whiteboard. Opus 5 also posted state-of-the-art results on frontier coding benchmarks and a perfect 42 out of 42 on the 2026 International Mathematical Olympiad problems.

Anthropic stripped 80% of Claude Code's built-in instructions for the launch, betting that better judgment beats thicker rulebooks. A major model release that resets price-to-performance expectations for AI tools developers are building on this week.

UK's Top State School Opens in Dubai
Queen Elizabeth's School, the UK's first state school, will open its first overseas campus in Dubai next month, charging annual tuition of nearly GBP 28,000, which is roughly the price of a new Nissan Micra every academic year, and will still likely earn a waitlist longer than a Friday brunch queue.

The Barnet institution will join a growing number of British schools exporting the national curriculum to the UAE, capitalising on parents' appetite for premium education with a recognisable stamp. The campus is the latest example of how Gulf families' spending on private schooling continues to reshape the international education market. The opening next month gives UAE-based parents a fresh option from a name-brand British school, just as the new academic year enrollment cycle heats up.
